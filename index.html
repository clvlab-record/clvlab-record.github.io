Why mispricing exists为什么错价存在

Not chance — structure.错价不是偶然，是结构。

No sharp money inside场内没有 sharp 的钱

Retail flow sets the price; the truest price sits outside — the sharp book with its margin removed.散户决定价格；最接近真值的价格在场外——去掉抽成的职业报价。

Thin liquidity流动性浅

A few hundred dollars move a price; nobody inside corrects it.几百美金就能推动价格，场内没有纠偏的力量。

Fees as a filter费用过滤器

Fees eat thin 1–2% gaps, so only thick deviations are flagged.手续费会吃掉 1–2% 的薄偏差，所以只报够厚的。

Two lines. One gap.两条线，一段距离。

Sharp no-vig line (conservative)公允价：场外 sharp 去抽成价（保守口径） Market price市价：预测市场价格

The gap is the edge. Prices don't always converge — the CLV at the close is what counts.两条线之间的距离就是 edge。价格不一定会收敛——以收盘时的 CLV 为准。

One needle, many backups一根针，多重备份

Quote sources are tiered and switch automatically — if one fails, the needle stays. Extra sources are redundancy, not confirmation.报价源按品质分级、自动切换，任何一个断了，针照样在。多源是为了容错，不是互相印证。

One factor: how far the market is from the no-vig price. No more, no less.唯一的因子：市价离 de-vig 公允价有多远。不多不少。
