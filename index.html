Why mispricing exists为什么错价存在

Not chance — structure.错价不是偶然，是结构。

No sharp money inside场内没有 sharp 的钱

Retail flow sets the price; the truest price sits outside — the sharp book with its margin removed.散户决定价格；最接近真值的价格在场外——去掉抽成的职业报价。

Thin liquidity流动性浅

A few hundred dollars move a price; nobody inside corrects it.几百美金就能推动价格，场内没有纠偏的力量。

Fees as a filter费用过滤器

Fees eat thin gaps, so only thick deviations are flagged.手续费会吃掉薄的偏差，所以只报够厚的。

Two lines. One gap.两条线，一段距离。

Sharp no-vig line (conservative)公允价：场外 sharp 去抽成价（保守口径） Market price市价：预测市场价格

The gap is the edge. Prices don't always converge — the CLV at the close is what counts.两条线之间的距离就是 edge。价格不一定会收敛——以收盘时的 CLV 为准。

FAQ常见问题

What fields does the data include?数据包含哪些字段？+

Signal time, market, side, public entry price, edge range, conservative CLV and the record's hash. No model internals.触发时间、市场、方向、公开进场价、edge 区间、保守口径 CLV，以及该条记录的 hash。不公开模型细节。

Do you sell these signals?你们卖信号吗？+

No. CLV Lab doesn't sell signals or tips. The channel and the record are public so the method can be judged by its numbers.不卖。CLV Lab 不卖推荐。频道与记录都是公开的，让方法用数字说话。

How do I use a signal?怎么使用信号？+

It is data, not advice. Compare the price you can get with the public entry — every minute and every tick shrinks the edge. Judge by CLV over many signals, never single results.这是数据，不是建议。先比较你拿得到的价格和公开进场价——每过一分钟、每差一格，优势都在缩小。看很多条信号的 CLV，不看单场输赢。
